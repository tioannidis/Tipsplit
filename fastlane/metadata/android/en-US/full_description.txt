Tipsplit is a simple, free and open-source tip calculator.

Enter the bill amount, pick a tip percentage (10, 15, 18, 20 or 25
percent, or a custom value), choose how many people split the bill, and
optionally round the total up to the next whole dollar. Tipsplit shows
the tip amount, the total, and the amount per person.

The app contains no ads, performs no tracking, requires no network
access and asks for no permissions. It is licensed under the GNU General
Public License v3.
